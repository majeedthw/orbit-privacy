Orbit privacy policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Orbit is an age calculator by Abdulmajeed Althuwaini.

Data kept on your device

Orbit stores your chosen birth date and app preferences in its local application storage. It uses those values to calculate ages, show birthday countdowns and apply your settings. There is no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking, or app-operated server receiving this information. Apple device backups may include local app data according to your device and iCloud backup settings. Orbit does not implement its own cloud synchronization.

Notifications

If you choose to enable birthday reminders, Orbit asks iOS for notification permission and schedules a local notification for your next birthday. No remote push service is involved. You can disable reminders in Orbit or manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

Sharing

Sharing is initiated by you. An exported age card shows your calculated age and, only if you turn off “Hide my birth date” (which is on by default), your birth date and next birthday. Data shared through another app or website is subject to the recipient’s and service’s practices. Orbit does not automatically share it. If you choose Save Image in the system share sheet, the exported card is saved to your photo library with the system’s permission.

Optional purchases

Orbit offers optional, one-time visual editions. Purchases are made and processed by Apple through the App Store; Orbit never sees or stores your payment details. On your device Orbit asks Apple’s StoreKit which editions your Apple Account owns, so your editions follow you to your other devices. Orbit has no server, receives no purchase history, and does not store ownership in its own data. Apple’s handling of purchases is described in Apple’s privacy policy.

Deletion

Use Settings → Delete my birthday to remove the stored birth date and its pending reminder. App preferences remain. Removing the app deletes its local application data, subject to your device’s backup behavior. Deleting local data does not remove copies that you have already exported or shared.

تبدأ المشاركة باختيارك فقط. تعرض البطاقة عمرك المحسوب، ولا تعرض تاريخ ميلادك وتاريخ عيد ميلادك القادم إلا إذا أوقفت خيار «إخفاء تاريخ ميلادي» (وهو مفعّل افتراضيًا). تخضع البيانات المشتركة عبر التطبيقات أو المواقع الأخرى لممارسات تلك الجهات. وعند اختيار حفظ الصورة من قائمة المشاركة، تُحفظ البطاقة في مكتبة الصور بعد إذن النظام.

يمكنك حذف تاريخ الميلاد والتذكير المجدول من الإعدادات، مع بقاء تفضيلات التطبيق. يؤدي حذف التطبيق إلى حذف بياناته المحلية بحسب آلية النسخ الاحتياطي في جهازك. لا يحذف ذلك النسخ التي سبق تصديرها أو مشاركتها.
